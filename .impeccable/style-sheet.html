Creative North Star: “The Observatory”

A dark-adapted instrument for looking at things that are hard to see: the passage inside a paper that answers the question, the row that does not belong, the count that contradicts the narrative. The surface stays dark so the content carries the light.

Do's and Don'ts

Do

  • Build depth from translucent #0f172a fills plus 1px hairlines.
  • One solid sky action per region; everything else at 10% sky or slate.
  • Dashed #334155 borders for empty states and drop zones.
  • tabular-nums on every numeric column.
  • Keep secondary copy at 12px or below.

Don't

  • Introduce a light theme or a second background hue.
  • Put a gradient anywhere except the “IF” mark.
  • Use indigo outside that gradient.
  • Add shadows to cards, buttons, or the sidebar.
  • Use a solid rose or emerald larger than a dot or chip.
  • Use pill shapes outside chips and status readouts.
